Four ways to trade the same chart.

Moving Average Crossover

Buys when the short-term average crosses above the long-term average, and sells on the reverse cross. A classic trend-following signal.

RSI Overbought / Oversold

Buys when momentum is stretched to the downside, sells when it's stretched to the upside. A contrarian, mean-reversion signal.

Combined Strategy

Only trades when a moving-average crossover and a confirming RSI reading agree — fewer trades, higher conviction.

Machine Learning

A linear regression model learns from price change, moving-average spread, and RSI to predict next-day returns.
